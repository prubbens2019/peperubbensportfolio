I wanted to track my workouts without an app full of features I never use. BOSfit is my answer: a sleek weekly planner for strength training, designed like a news site. It was a fun exercise in how much character you can give a simple tool.

My starting point was that tracking has to be so light that you actually keep doing it. You should be able to tick off an exercise with one tap, in the middle of the gym. That is why BOSfit consists of three screens: workouts, stats and settings, protected with a PIN code.

The overview (image 1) borrows its design from a news site. At the top is a ticker with the week's progress, below it a card per workout with a photo, quote and progress bar. You can activate, pause and reorder workouts.

In a workout (image 2) you find the exercises with sets, reps and weight, which you tick off while training. A small dot next to the weight shows whether you lifted heavier, the same or lighter than before, so you see your progress without doing the maths yourself.

At the end of the week (image 3) the app records your progress. You see your total score, your recent form and, per week, how much of each workout you completed, including the average weight progression. That turns a list of exercises into an instructive logbook. The app is built in React with Firebase, so everything stays the same on phone and laptop.
