At BlueDot Foundation (bluedot.nl), the design label by and for TU Delft students, I served as Commissioner of Sales. My main task was developing the sales strategy: not just getting a student design to market, but making sure it actually sold.

One concrete example: at one point we sold 52 fruit bowls from the label l'offre as part of a Christmas hamper. A moment like that shows exactly what BlueDot is about. A student design that sounds good on paper only really succeeds once it reaches a sales channel and people actually buy it.

What I enjoyed most about the board role was the combination of carrying responsibility for my own part of the organisation while working alongside other board members. You are accountable for your own results, but the whole only works if everyone does their part well.

BlueDot mainly taught me what sales looks like in practice when the product does not come from a large company, but from a student who just graduated. It is not about a marketing budget or an established brand, but about persuasiveness and the willingness to pick up the phone yourself.
