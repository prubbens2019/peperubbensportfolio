At Il Tartufo, an Italian delicatessen and restaurant on the Minderbroerstraat in Delft, just behind the Oude Kerk, I worked for years front-of-house and later also in the kitchen. The place drew a loyal lunch crowd and regularly hosted evenings with live jazz, so it stayed busy.

I eventually became evening lead of the service team. Mostly a matter of keeping an overview when the place filled up and the menu started running thin at the same time.
