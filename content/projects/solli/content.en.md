Applying by job title works poorly when the jobs you are looking for have no clear title. For work in strategy, product and innovation I therefore came up with Solli: start from what you enjoy and let the vacancies come to that. A taste test, a profile and a swipe feed. I have become quite enthusiastic about it.

Many young professionals look for work in a field with vague titles: strategist, innovation manager, product, programme. A search bar helps little there, because you do not know which word to type. My hypothesis was that it works better to start with the person.

The taste test (image 1) consists of four rounds with twenty short questions in total: silly questions, sliders, fictional mini job postings and a practical part. Each round has its own colour, so you feel you are making progress, and there are no right or wrong answers.

The answers produce a taste profile (image 2). It shows what gives you energy, such as designing, figuring things out or people, and how you prefer to work: fast or thoughtful, with a framework or a blank sheet. The profile shifts with every swipe you make later.

In the feed (image 3) you swipe on vacancies: yes, hmm or no, with a match score and the reasons behind it. What you save, you follow in the tracker, a kanban board from saved to interview and offer. Solli is built with Next.js and TypeScript, as a PWA. The vacancies in these mockups are fictional.
